# UI audit 2026-10-11: tenant portal and admin, GB and PK

Find and prove only. No app code was changed. Branch `audit/2026-10-11-ui` (from `origin/main` 97412ae).
Server-side findings each have a Pest test in `tests/Feature/Audit/UiAuditTest.php` (group `audit-ui`) that
**fails until the finding is fixed**. Front-end-only findings give file:line and a repro.

## Summary

The crawl did not find a 500, a missing deferred prop, or a JS console error on any page. That held for 1280px and
375px, in GB and PK, with demo data and with an empty business. The findings below are throttling, layout, billing
consistency, validation and wording.

## How it was run

- Scratch SQLite DBs inside the worktree (`storage/audit/gb.sqlite`, `storage/audit/pk.sqlite`, not committed):
  `migrate`, `db:seed`, `demo:seed --days=30`, `demo:billing --scenario=all` (GB; PK has no Direct Debit showcase),
  and a test admin from `admin:create`. Two `php artisan serve` instances ran: COUNTRY=GB on :8711 and COUNTRY=PK
  on :8712. The main checkout, its database and the servers were not touched.
- **Crawl.** Every admin and app GET route from `route:list --json` was visited on both instances, 229 visits each
  over 181 routes. Parameters came from the scratch DB, and all 10 reports, every purchasing kind and every news kind
  were included. The crawl checked HTTP status and the Inertia `data-page` props (component, `errors`). It also
  re-requested every deferred prop group as a partial reload and checked for missing keys. Every email template
  preview listed on each instance (GB 23, PK 21) was opened as well.
- **Render.** The Claude Browser pane rendered each page in a same-origin iframe, with console.error, `error` and
  `unhandledrejection` hooked before the app's scripts ran. For each page it recorded blank renders, visible
  `NaN`/`undefined`/`null`/`Invalid Date`, horizontal overflow, unlabelled inputs and nameless buttons, plus UK words
  on PK and PK words on GB. The passes were:
  - Tenant pages: GB at 1280px (127 pages) and 375px (112), PK at 1280px (125).
  - Admin pages: GB at 1280px (48, including every tenant tab and the 5 billing demo businesses) and 375px (25),
    PK at 1280px (43).
  - Empty business (Corner Shop Express, no demo data): GB, 92 tenant pages.
- **Manual flows** (browser and JSON calls):
  - Change plan preview.
  - Email settings: every category held, then `alerts:digest` and `billing:run` to make held emails, then preview,
    send and discard.
  - Licence settings: "Customise for this shop", the custom features badge, then "Use the plan's features".
  - Add till with a per-till setup fee, including the invalid-amount message.
  - PK till settings save: wallets, quick cash and currency sign.
  - Downloads: CSV exports, invoice and statement PDFs (checked with pdftotext), report print.
  - AI assistant status.

## Findings

### UI-1 (Medium): every `throttle:N,1` route shares one per-user counter, so normal use makes exports answer 429

- **Page/flow:** Sales → Export, Reports → Export CSV, Timesheets export, Transfers export, Activity export, Admin
  audit-log export, invoice PDFs, statement PDF. In the crawl, four exports answered **429** on their first hit, on
  both instances.
- **What:** Laravel's `ThrottleRequests` keys an unnamed limiter by user id only (`sha1(user id)`; route and limit
  are not part of the key, see `vendor/laravel/framework/src/Illuminate/Routing/Middleware/ThrottleRequests.php:98`
  and `:222`). The 80 `throttle:` uses in `routes/app.php` and the 26 in `routes/admin.php` therefore count against
  one bucket per user. Each route then compares that shared count with its own limit. Here is how that plays out:
  - 10 throttled hits in a minute, from any route, lock every `throttle:10,1` route: sales export, activity export,
    privacy export, admin audit-log export, staff PIN.
  - 30 hits lock the `throttle:30,1` routes: report CSV, transfers CSVs, customer statement PDF, sales export
    download.
  - The product import page polls every 2 s (`resources/js/pages/app/products/imports/show.tsx:24`), which is 30
    hits a minute on its own.
  - Admin search (`throttle:120,1`) is called as the admin types.
- **Proof:** `UI-1: browsing other throttled pages does not use up the sales export limit` (10 views of "Add
  product", then the export answers 429) and `UI-1: polling an import page does not lock the user out of the report
  exports` (30 import-list hits, then the report CSV answers 429).
- **Fix:** Give each throttle its own key, either with the third `prefix` parameter (`throttle:10,1,sales-export`)
  or with named limiters (`RateLimiter::for('exports', fn ($r) => Limit::perMinute(10)->by($r->user()->id.'|'.$r->route()->getName()))`).
  Add a test that one route's limit is unaffected by traffic on another.
- **Risk to live customers:** UK owners and managers who export or print after a few edits or a running import get
  "Too many requests" for up to a minute. The cause is hard to diagnose, and PK is the same.

### UI-2 (Medium): the Licences tab can move every till to another plan with no billing change, and support admins can do it

- **Page/flow:** Admin → business → Licences → "Change the plan for new tills" with "also move the live licences"
  (`resources/js/components/admin/licences/tenant-licences-panel.tsx:203-212`, `PUT /admin/tenants/{company}/plan`,
  `routes/admin.php:196`, gate `licences.manage`).
- **What:** This route is older than the 2026-10-07 "Change plan" (Billing tab: preview, setup fee, recurring fee,
  invoices, Direct Debit, `billing.plan_changed`, gate `billing.manage`). It still sets `companies.plan_id` and moves
  every live licence through `ChangeCompanyPlan`, and that path touches none of the billing. A **support** admin
  (`licences.manage` but not `billing.manage`, `app/Domain/Admin/Enums/AdminRole.php:59`) can therefore put a
  business on Pro features and leave it paying the Standard fee, with no preview and no billing audit.
  DECISIONS.md "Change plan" says the Billing tab path is the way a business changes plan.
- **Proof:** `UI-2: the licences tab cannot move every till to another plan without the billing change`. A support
  admin gets 302 success, both licences end up on Pro, and no `billing.plan_changed` is recorded.
- **Fix:** Pick one of these:
  - (a) Remove "apply to licences" from the Licences tab and keep "plan for new tills" only when the business has no
    live tills.
  - (b) Route the Licences tab action through `ChangeBusinessPlan` with the same preview, and gate it with
    `billing.manage`.

  Then update `LicenceAdminScreensTest` ("renew all and the company plan are changed from the tenant page"), which
  currently expects support to be able to do this.
- **Risk to live customers:** Revenue leakage and inconsistent invoices. The business is not harmed.

### UI-3 (Low): Pakistan admin email settings still describe Direct Debit emails

- **Page:** PK `/admin/settings/emails`.
- **What:** The "Reminders and notices" category says "...Direct Debit setup, failed and cancelled..."
  (`app/Domain/Mail/Enums/EmailCategory.php:36`). The page description also says "...suspensions and Direct Debit
  carry on exactly the same..." (`resources/js/pages/admin/settings/emails.tsx:49`). PK has no Direct Debit (P5).
- **Proof:** `UI-3: Pakistan admin email settings do not describe Direct Debit emails`. The browser text scan of the
  PK admin found both strings.
- **Fix:** Take both texts from the country profile (`Country::billing.collection`). GB stays byte-identical.
- **Risk:** Admin staff only (wording).

### UI-4 (Medium): PK till setting "Quick cash buttons" accepts anything and pushes it to the tills

- **Page/flow:** PK `/app/settings` → Payments → Quick cash buttons.
- **What:** The setting is free text, max 60 characters (`config/country.php:224`). Saving `abc,xyz` works and
  returns no validation error. It was stored in `till_settings` and audited ("Payments.quick cash:
  100,500,1000,5000,10000 → abc,xyz"), and it will be pulled by every PK till. `-5,0` and `100,,500` are accepted too.
- **Proof:** `UI-4: Pakistan till settings refuse quick cash buttons that are not amounts`
  (`SettingCatalogue::normalise` accepts all three). The repro was done live on the PK server with
  `PUT /app/settings` and returned 303 with no errors.
- **Fix:** Add a `moneyList` type (or a `pattern`) to the catalogue that allows 1 to 6 positive whole or 2-decimal
  amounts, comma separated, and gives a clear message ("Quick cash buttons: amounts separated by commas, for example
  100,500,1000"). Ask the EPOS team how the till parses an invalid value.
- **Risk:** PK live tills may show broken quick cash buttons or reject the setting. GB is unaffected (no such
  setting).

### UI-5 (Medium): an overdue business gets no portal warning before suspension

- **Page:** `/app` (and every app page) for a business in status `overdue`.
- **What:** The shared `billingNotice` covers only the Direct Debit set-up deadline (`MandateDeadline::state`,
  `app/Http/Middleware/HandleInertiaRequests.php:118-129`). An overdue business is suspended after 7 days and its
  tills lock, yet nothing tells it so outside the Billing page. The demo business "Khan Mini Mart" (overdue) showed
  `billingNotice: null` on every page and was then suspended by `billing:run`. With P11 an admin can also hold the
  "Reminders and notices" emails, and then the owner has no warning at all.
- **Proof:** `UI-5: an overdue business sees a warning in the portal before its tills lock`.
- **Fix:** Extend `billingNotice` (and `app-billing-banner.tsx`) with an "overdue" state: the amount due, the lock
  date, and a link to Billing for `billing.manage` users and "ask the owner" for others. Test it on GB and PK.
- **Risk:** Paying UK customers can be locked out at the till without any on-screen warning in the portal.

### UI-6 (Medium): pages wider than the screen, because grid and flex children cannot shrink below a wide table

- **Pages (measured `scrollWidth - clientWidth`):**
  - Desktop 1280px: `/app/accounts/export` +585px (GB and PK; the whole page, header, filters and alerts run off to
    the right), `/app/sales/{id}` +61px GB and +105px PK (Payments card).
  - Phone 375px:
    - `/app/staff/time/rota` +603px
    - `/app/accounts/export` +1226px
    - `/app/stock/products/{id}` +242px
    - `/app/cash/shifts/{id}` +228px
    - `/app/prices/{id}` +154px (chart)
    - `/app/compliance/diary` +136px
    - `/app/accounts` +118px
    - `/app/accounts/vat` +96px
    - `/app/products/categories` +51px
    - `/app/accounts/trial-balance` +42px
    - `/app/sales/{id}` +38px
    - Admin: business Billing tab +28px
- **What:** Page bodies are `<div className="grid gap-6">{children}</div>` with no column template, so a grid item
  has `min-width: auto` and is as wide as its widest content. A table inside `overflow-x-auto` (rota
  `min-w-[960px]`, the export's "file's first rows" table with `whitespace-nowrap` cells) or a `truncate` line then
  forces the whole column wide instead of scrolling inside its card. Where it occurs:
  - `resources/js/components/app/accounts/accounts-page.tsx:206`, `staff-time/time-page.tsx:198`,
    `cash/cash-page.tsx:169`, `compliance/compliance-page.tsx:174`, `calendar/calendar-page.tsx:136`,
    `pharmacy/format.tsx:92`.
  - `resources/js/pages/app/sales/show.tsx:64` (`grid gap-4`) with `components/app/sales/receipt-side.tsx:52`
    (`truncate` in a flex row without `min-w-0`).
  - `resources/js/pages/app/accounts/export.tsx:182-206`.
- **Repro:** Open `/app/accounts/export` at 1280px. The page scrolls sideways and "Download for Xero" is off-screen.
  Open `/app/staff/time/rota` at 375px and the cards are cut on the right (screenshot taken during the audit).
- **Fix:** Use `grid grid-cols-1` (`minmax(0,1fr)`) or add `min-w-0` to the children in the shared page wrappers
  (`[&>*]:min-w-0`), and add `min-w-0` to the flex parent of `truncate` lines. Add a visual or Playwright check at
  375px if one is introduced.
- **Risk:** Real UK customers on phones and laptops see broken layouts on accounts, rota, cash and stock pages. This
  fails BRAND.md "phone-width layout".

### UI-7 (Medium): the Age checks page is slow (2.6 to 9 s)

- **Page:** `/app/compliance/age-checks` (default filters).
- **What:** It took 4.1 s (GB) and 2.6 s (PK) in the crawl and 8.4 to 9.4 s on repeated PK loads, with only 30 days
  of demo sales (82k sale lines). Every other page was under 1.6 s. `AgeChecks::checks()`
  (`app/Domain/Compliance/Queries/AgeChecks.php:82-95`) joins `sale_lines` → `sales` → `products` filtered on
  `sale_lines.is_age_restricted`. No index covers that column, and the query runs once per breakdown (shop, staff,
  rule, top products, totals). One such query alone takes about 1 s on SQLite.
- **Proof:** curl timings on the PK server (`time_total` 8.89 s and 9.43 s). The query plan uses
  `sale_lines_company_id_product_id_index (company_id=?)` and then scans every line of the business.
- **Fix:** Use one pass that pre-aggregates checks per (shop, staff, rule, product) for the period, or use
  `rpt_product_daily` if it carries age-restricted flags. Add an index such as
  `sale_lines(company_id, is_age_restricted, sale_id)` and check the plan on MySQL with a year of data.
- **Risk:** It grows with history. A busy UK shop with a year of sales may hit gateway timeouts.

### UI-8 (Low): no React error boundary, so one render error blanks the whole portal

- **Where:** `resources/js/app.tsx:26-28` renders `<App>` with no error boundary, and none exists anywhere
  (`grep ErrorBoundary` finds nothing).
- **What:** No render error occurred in this audit. But a single bad prop (for example a till sending an unexpected
  value) unmounts the whole app and leaves a white page with no way back except a reload.
- **Fix:** Wrap the page in a boundary inside the layouts that shows "This page could not be shown" with a reload
  button, the trace id, and a report to the server log.
- **Risk:** Low today. It reduces the impact of future bugs.

### UI-10 (Low): PK plan form drops the "yearly saving" hint at Rs 100,000 and above

- **Where:** `resources/js/components/admin/plans/plan-format.ts:47`, where `yearlySaving` parses with
  `/^(\d{1,5})(?:\.(\d{1,2}))?$/`.
- **Repro:** PK `/admin/plans/create`. Set monthly to 10,000 and yearly to 1,00,000. Nothing appears under the
  yearly price (GB amounts never reach 6 digits). The inputs themselves accept it (`moneyInputPattern()` was fixed in

- **Fix:** Reuse the profile-aware pattern from `lib/country.ts:164`.
- **Risk:** Admin staff only.

### UI-11 (Low): admin licence wording

- **Where:** Admin → business → Branches and tills.
  - The branch row shows "2 of 2 till keys in use **Full**" (meaning no free keys) right next to "Trial", while
    "Full" is also the name of a licence kind in the same dialog.
  - The Licence settings dialog lists each feature with its raw key under the label ("Second screen /
    second_screen").
- **Fix:** Change the badge to "No keys free" or "At limit". Show the raw keys only on hover, or drop them.
- **Risk:** Staff confusion only.

### UI-12 (Low): the activity log shows setting keys, not their labels

- **Where:** `/app/activity` and its CSV.
- **What:** Till settings changes read "Payments.quick cash: ... → ...; Payments.allow wallets: — → false;
  Shop.currency symbol: — → Rs." The page has the labels ("Quick cash buttons", "Phone wallets", "Currency sign").
- **Fix:** Map setting keys through `SettingCatalogue::all()[$key]['label']` when the audit change is formatted.
- **Risk:** Cosmetic. Owners read these logs.

### UI-13 (Low): PK demo and seed data are UK-flavoured

- **What:** On a COUNTRY=PK instance:
  - `demo:seed` creates "Leeds"/"Bradford" shops (the UI maps the sample places, but not the stored names).
  - It creates promotions named "2 for £3", "Challenge 25" training topics, "News UK" publishers, ledger accounts
    "VAT on sales" / "VAT on purchases", and GB-format VAT numbers that print as "STRN GB…" on invoices.
  - `PlanSeeder` creates GBP plans, shown as "Pounds sterling (GBP)" on `/admin/plans/{id}`. The cause is
    `Plan::CURRENCY` in `database/seeders/PlanSeeder.php:46`.
- **Fix:** Have the demo builders and `PlanSeeder` take names, currency and tax wording from the country profile.
- **Risk:** None to live data (both refuse production). Sales demos on the Pakistan instance look British.

## What was fine

- **Errors.** No 500 anywhere, and no Laravel `ERROR` in the log during the crawl. Every deferred prop group came
  back complete. There was no `NaN`, `undefined`, `Invalid Date` or `[object Object]` on any rendered page.
- **PK wording.** PK pages, exports and PDFs use Rs, lakh grouping, GST and STRN/NTN wording, and Asia/Karachi times
  ("Time (Pakistan)" in the activity CSV). There was no `£`, VAT, HMRC, postcode or Companies House text in
  app-generated PK UI. The only exceptions are UI-3 and the demo data in UI-13. The HMRC VAT return tab and routes
  are hidden on PK.
- **GB wording.** No PK words (Rs, rupee, NTN, STRN, JazzCash, Easypaisa, lakh) appear anywhere on GB pages.
- **Change plan preview** (JSON) reads well and wrote nothing.
- **Email control.** Holding categories, held digest and suspension emails, preview (sandboxed CSP), send and discard
  all work. A demo business is never emailed.
- **Custom features.** "Customise for this shop" → badge "Custom features (differ from the plan)" → "Use the plan's
  features" confirm dialog → badge gone.
- **Add till with a per-till setup fee.** It shows "Invoiced now: £1,440.00 incl. VAT. Empty = £1,200.00 per till ×
  1 till". An invalid amount shows an inline message and does not submit.
- **PK settings.** The PK till settings page shows currency sign, phone wallets, quick cash and WhatsApp code with
  rupee defaults, and hides the UK-only settings. The PK product form offers only None and 18 or over.
- **Accessibility.** Every input and Radix checkbox has a label (`label[for]` or `aria-label`). The nameless buttons
  the scan flagged were Radix's hidden native inputs.
- **States.** The account-on-hold page shows for a suspended business. All empty states have text.
