Scope: `app/Domain/Billing` (with GoCardless), Plans, onboarding billing, setup fee (per business / per till, covered
tills, overrides, instalments), added-till charges, change plan, invoices, payments, VAT and rounding, `billing:run`,
overdue / suspension, licence effects, Direct Debit, Pakistan manual billing, held emails, stats, admin and tenant
billing screens, PDFs. Read only: no app code was changed.

Proof: `tests/Feature/Audit/BillingAuditTest.php` (14 tests, all green, SQLite). Each `BILL-n` test **reproduces**
the problem: it asserts what the code does today, with a comment saying what it should do. When a finding is fixed,
flip those expectations. The `fine:` tests pin what was checked and is correct, on an **Istanbul-like fixture**
(`auditIstanbul()`): plan "Setup only" (per till since P11, £1,200 a till), 2 tills, onboarded on Direct Debit mode
with the 3-day mandate deadline long gone, a bank-transfer setup fee payment reversed and its invoice voided, a second
wrong invoice paid, reversed and voided, then one setup fee invoice 2 × £750 + VAT = £1,800 paid in cash,
`till_setup_fee_override` 750, 2 covered tills, the shop's own features with `cloud_sync`.

Run: `php vendor/bin/pest tests/Feature/Audit/BillingAuditTest.php` (about 8 s).

## BILL-1 (High): a stale Direct Debit deadline suspends a setup-only business as soon as anything recurs

**What.** The wizard and trial approval put every UK business on Direct Debit mode with `mandate_deadline_at` = now + 3
days, setup-only plans included (`OnboardTenantBilling.php:38-42`). While nothing recurs the deadline is dormant
(`MandateDeadline::applies`, `MandateDeadline.php:30-37`, needs a non-zero `SubscriptionAmount`). Once something
recurs, `MandateDeadline::missed` (`:40-44`) compares against that old date, so `EnforceDirectDebit` (`:61-62`)
suspends at the next 06:00 run ("No Direct Debit set up"): no reminder (the reminder only fires before the deadline,
`:73-89`), no new deadline; the portal banner appears already at "0 days left". A hand payment cannot lift it (`ReleaseBillingHolds.php:55`).
Change plan starts a new deadline (`PlanChangePlanner.php:62-63`), but these paths do not:

- (a) Billing tab → Change pricing: a monthly price on a setup-only business (`UpdateCompanyPricing.php:41-66` saves
  and syncs, nothing about the deadline). Plausible for Istanbul: the owner's open question is a cloud-sync add-on.
- (b) The business's plan is made inactive (what the archive refusal tells the admin to do). New tills are licensed on
  `DefaultPlan::for` (`DefaultPlan.php:18-29`, used by `IssueLicenceForNewTill.php:35`), which skips an inactive plan
  and falls back to the portal default. A third Istanbul till lands on e.g. "Setup + monthly" (£12 a month), per-till
  pricing keeps each till's own plan price, and the business recurs. The same `DefaultPlan::for` also drives
  `SetupFeeTills::perTill` (`SetupFeeTills.php:40`) and `SetupFee::planFee/perTillFee` (`SetupFee.php:23,32`), while
  `ApplySetupFeeTerms::planType` uses the licences' plan (`ApplySetupFeeTerms.php:96-101`): the added-till charge and
  the plan type can disagree.

**Who.** Istanbul (deadline 9 Oct, passed) and every UK setup-only business onboarded through the wizard. PK is not
affected (no deadline on PK).

**Proof.** `BILL-1` (price override → suspended on 12 Oct, 0 reminders, tills `suspended`), `BILL-1b` (plan inactive →
third till on "Setup + monthly", £14.40 recurring → suspended).

**Suggested fix.** Treat a deadline as starting when recurring starts: when `SubscriptionAmount` goes from £0 to more
(pricing override, till on a recurring plan, cycle change), set a new `mandate_deadline_at` = now + deadline days and
clear `mandate_reminder_for`, as `ChangeBusinessPlan` does. Cheapest guard: in `MandateDeadline::applies`, ignore a
deadline set before the business first recurred (store `recurring_since`). For (b), license new tills on the plan the
business's live tills are on (`CompanyPricing::for(...)->plan`) and use the same plan in `SetupFeeTills::perTill` and
`SetupFee`.

**Risk for live customers.** Low if the fix only moves deadlines forward; it must not lift or create suspensions on
deploy. Check on live first: `billing_accounts` with `billing_mode = directDebit`, no usable mandate and a past
`mandate_deadline_at` (Istanbul is one) — none of them recurs today, so none is suspended now.

## BILL-2 (Medium): voiding an unpaid setup fee invoice to correct it gives 10 free years

**What.** `SetupFeeState::for` ignores draft and void setup fee invoices (`SetupFeeState.php:47-50`). With all of them
void and `setup_fee_invoiced_at` set it returns `none` = settled (`:66-67`). "Void and re-issue" (`VoidInvoice.php:73,
109-130`) leaves the corrected invoice as a draft, so until it is issued the fee reads settled and billing:run
(`RefreshSetupFeeTerms` → `ApplySetupFeeTerms::setupOnly`, `ApplySetupFeeTerms.php:105-108`) writes the 10-year licence.
Issuing the draft makes the fee "unpaid" again, but the 10 years stay (a paid date is never shortened). Recurring plans:
the Direct Debit subscription may start (`isStarted`) before the fee is paid.

**Who.** Any setup-only business whose unpaid setup fee invoice is corrected; Istanbul's corrections happened on paid
invoices (payments reversed first, then voided), same mechanism: had billing:run run between the void and the
re-issue, it would have extended for free (harmless there, as it was paid in the end).

**Proof.** `BILL-2`.

**Suggested fix.** Count a draft that `replaces_invoice_id` a void setup fee invoice as unpaid in `SetupFeeState`, and
treat "every setup fee invoice void" as waived only when the admin chose void without a corrected draft (or record
the waiver explicitly, e.g. `setup_fee_waived_at`).

**Risk.** Low; it only makes the state stricter. Check live for businesses whose setup fee invoices are all void
(state `none`) before deploying, so none flips to "unpaid" unexpectedly (Istanbul has a paid one: not affected).

## BILL-3 (Medium): a late-failed Direct Debit payment that is retried and collected is never recorded

**What.** `ApplyGoCardlessPayment` records a payment only while `payment_id` is null (`ApplyGoCardlessPayment.php:76`).
A late failure (confirmed → failed) reverses the recorded payment but keeps `payment_id` (`:157-167`); the retry
(pending) resets only the email stamps (`:62-64`). When GoCardless (Success+, or the staff Retry button) collects it,
`payment_id` is still set, so nothing is recorded; and even with it cleared, `RecordPayment`'s gateway dedupe
(`RecordPayment.php:60-66`) would return the reversed payment as a duplicate. The invoice stays owed, goes overdue and
the business is suspended although it paid. The reconcile replays the same path, so it does not heal it.

**Who.** UK Direct Debit businesses (none paying by DD among real customers yet; Istanbul is setup-only).

**Proof.** `BILL-3` (confirmed → failed → pending → confirmed; invoice £60 owed; suspended 19 Nov).

**Suggested fix.** On a retry of a payment whose recorded payment is reversed, clear `payment_id`; make the gateway
dedupe ignore reversed payments (or key it by payment id + attempt). Add the late-failure path to the DD tests.

**Risk.** Low; touches only payments already reversed. Make sure a re-delivered `confirmed` without a failure in
between still records once.

## BILL-7 (Medium, PK): paid after the period ended → nothing renewed, never invoiced again

**What.** `SettleInvoice::renewLicences` renews only while the period end is ahead (`SettleInvoice.php:118`). The next
invoice is only made while the tills' paid date is within `days_before` behind (`IssueManualInvoices.php:46,79`;
UK cash mode `GenerateDueInvoices.php:41,67`). A November invoice paid on 2 December: invoice paid, suspension lifted,
the tills stay paid to 31 Oct (expired) and no December invoice ever comes. The customer paid for a month spent
suspended and has nothing to pay to unlock.

**Who.** Pakistan (every period is a hand-paid invoice; late payment is likely). UK businesses on upfront/cash mode
alike (drafts). Not UK Direct Debit (SubscriptionInvoices handles it).

**Proof.** `BILL-7` (group `country-pk`).

**Suggested fix.** When an invoice is paid after its period, either renew to the end of the current period from the
payment day, or have `IssueManualInvoices` issue the next period for any business with nothing open and tills expired
(no `since` cut-off once a payment came in). Owner decision on what a late payer gets.

**Risk.** Medium for PK customers: changing it renews or invoices businesses that are expired today. Run a dry run on
the PK server first (businesses with live tills expired more than 7 days and no open invoice).

## BILL-4 (Low): demo money in the live billing figures

**What.** `BillingStats::for` (`BillingStats.php:39-59`: cash due, overdue, due this week, collected this month,
drafts, credit held, billing-suspended) has no `is_demo` filter; `demo:billing` runs on live with `--force` and makes
paid and failed demo invoices. Demo invoices are numbered `DEMO-INV-…`, so they are recognisable, but the totals are not
real money. **Proof:** `BILL-4`. **Fix:** `whereHas('company', fn ($q) => $q->where('is_demo', false))` on each sum (or
a shared scope). **Risk:** none (read only).

## BILL-5 (Low): the setup fee "paid by … on …" line is from a reversed payment

**What.** `RecordUpfrontPayment` stamps `upfront_amount`, `upfront_method`, `upfront_recorded_at` once, with the first
payment (`RecordUpfrontPayment.php:109-115,135-142`); reversing that payment or voiding the invoice never updates them.
The admin Payment settings (`pricing-panel.tsx:93-98`, from `DirectDebitData.php:80-85`) and the portal billing page
(`pages/app/billing.tsx:81-84`, from `PortalBilling.php:60-65`) show them. Istanbul's owner sees "£1,800.00 Paid by bank
transfer · 6 Oct" while the payment that stands is cash on 8 Oct (and `upfront_amount` still says £1,440). A setup fee
that ends "none" after a void shows the stale `upfront_amount` next to "Nothing to pay". **Proof:** `BILL-5`. **Fix:**
show the method and date of the payments allocated to the paid setup fee invoices (`SetupFeeState`), and keep the stamp
only as "recorded". **Risk:** display only.

## BILL-6 (Low): a setup fee settled by credit unlocks nothing until billing:run

**What.** Only `RecordPayment` calls `ApplySetupFeeTerms(justPaid)` (`RecordPayment.php:131-133`). A setup fee invoice
settled by a credit note (`IssueCreditNote.php:96-103`), by "Apply credit" (`ApplyCredit.php:36-50`) or by credit when
issued (`IssueInvoice.php:88-94`) does not: a setup-only till stays on trial (locked if the trial ended) until the next
06:00 run; on PK the first period invoice and the trial bridge wait; on the UK the subscription and `bridgeTrial` wait.
**Proof:** `BILL-6`. **Fix:** call `ApplySetupFeeTerms(justPaid: true)` after any settlement of a setup-fee kind
(e.g. from `SettleInvoice`'s callers, after the transaction). **Risk:** low.

## BILL-8 (Low, operational): a paid invoice cannot be corrected in the admin

**What.** `VoidInvoice` refuses paid invoices (`VoidInvoice.php:53-58`) and no admin route reverses or refunds a
recorded payment (`routes/admin.php:277-313`; `ReversePayment` is only called for GoCardless). DECISIONS 1.8 "Open
items (1)" lists it. Istanbul's corrections (PAY-000001..3 reversed, INV-000002/3 voided) had to be done by hand on the
server. **Proof:** `BILL-8`. **Fix:** an audited "Reverse payment" (billing.manage, reason required, the
`ReversePayment` action, then optionally void) and a credit/refund note for money given back. **Risk:** new feature;
keep chargeback semantics (no credit) separate from "recorded by mistake" (money never received).

## BILL-9 (Low): no idempotency on recording payments

**What.** `RecordPayment` dedupes only gateway payments (`RecordPayment.php:60-66`); `RecordUpfrontPayment` pays the
next unpaid instalment on each call (`RecordUpfrontPayment.php:63-64,94-107`). A resubmitted form (double click,
browser retry) records a second payment: a second instalment paid, or credit on the business. **Proof:** `BILL-9`.
**Fix:** an idempotency key from the form (hidden UUID, unique per company) checked under the billing lock. **Risk:**
low.

## BILL-10 (Low, by reading): change plan is not re-checked under the lock

**What.** `ChangeBusinessPlan::handle` builds the plan (`check()` → `PlanChangePlanner::plan`) before the transaction
and does not re-check `samePlan` after `accounts->lock()` (`ChangeBusinessPlan.php:70-105`). Two confirms at the same
time (throttle allows 10 a minute) can both apply: two setup fee invoices, or two first-period invoices
(`allowOverlap: true`). Not reproduced (needs two processes); a sequential second confirm is refused correctly.
**Fix:** re-plan (or re-check `samePlan`) inside the transaction after the lock. **Risk:** none.

## Checked and found fine

- **Istanbul-like data** (`fine:` tests): setup fee state `paid` £1,800 (void invoices and reversed payments ignored;
  reversed payments hold no credit); plan type setup-only; recurring £0; mandate deadline not applied; 10-year
  licences, `active`, `cloud_sync` kept; a year of billing:run runs: no invoice, no overdue, no suspension, top-up once
  below 9 years; admin tenant page, billing overview, invoice pages and PDFs (paid and void), payment pages (reversed
  and kept) and the portal billing page render.
- **Third till for Istanbul**: one "Setup fee (added tills)" invoice, £750 + £150 VAT, one line, covered tills 3, the
  till held on trial (billing:run does not extend it), paid → 10-year licence.
- **Change plan for Istanbul**: to "Setup + monthly" no setup fee (2 covered), a new mandate deadline; to another
  setup-only plan no invoice, licences kept at 10 years, own features kept, not suspended.
- **Money**: bcmath everywhere, VAT per line half away from zero, totals = sum of lines, credit notes split at the
  invoice rate, `InvoiceBalance` from allocations and credit notes, never below 0. Instalments add up to the quoted
  gross (the net may differ from the quoted net by a penny, e.g. £1,000 in 3 parts → £999.99 net + £200.01 VAT; HMRC
  per-line rounding, acceptable).
- **Payments**: allocation never exceeds the payment or the balance; void releases allocations to credit; reversal
  releases allocations with no credit, is once per payment, reopens with `reopened_at` so the 7-day suspension counts
  from the reversal; GoCardless gateway payments recorded once under the billing lock.
- **Numbering**: `DocumentNumbers` increments inside the issuing transaction (existing parallel-process test); demo
  documents use their own counters.
- **Overdue / suspension**: invoices still being collected by GoCardless are not marked overdue; one suspension per
  invoice; only billing's own suspension is lifted; cancelled and suspended businesses are left alone.
- **Webhooks**: signature checked, each event stored once (unique id), handlers fetch the current state, the payment
  row is unique per GoCardless id.
- **Demo and PK guards**: `DemoSafeGoCardlessClient` refuses demo ids and demo metadata; `SyncSubscription` returns
  `demo` / `noMandate`; PK binds `NoDirectDebitClient`, onboards without a deadline, Direct Debit routes 404.
- **Held emails**: `BrandedMailable` checks once (queue / later / send) after the demo suppression; admin sends run
  inside `EmailControl::manually()`; a held invoice email does not count as sent; void invoices cannot be sent;
  licence keys are only mailed by an admin. With every category off, billing:run's emails (suspension, reminders,
  invoices) are held, not sent (by reading; covered by `tests/Feature/Mail/EmailControlTest.php`).
- **Edge data** (by reading): no plan → £0 lines, no crash; an archived plan cannot be archived while in use; deleted or revoked
  licences are skipped by SettleInvoice and setup-only terms.
