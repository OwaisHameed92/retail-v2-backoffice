## 1. Your questions

1. **`linesJson` shape: keep yours.** camelCase with `lineId` is right; please don't copy our table. The till stores lines
   in its own internal form and converts yours when it applies the row.
   - The till reads: `lineId` (keep it stable across versions; the till compares lines by it), `externalLineId`,
     `productId` (parts only; our product id), `name`, `qty` (> 0), `unitPrice` (≥ 0, inc VAT), `vatPercentage`,
     `lineType` (`part` | `labour` | `custom` | `fee`). 1–200 lines.
   - The till does **not** read `vatRateId`, `lineTotal` or `vatAmount`. VAT is charged at the line's `vatPercentage`,
     as for jobs the till finds itself. The till works the totals out itself; if they differ from `goodsTotal`, the job
     shows on the till's Problems tab and the customer pays `goodsTotal`.
   - Members the till doesn't know are ignored, so you can add more later.
   - A list the till cannot read refuses the whole row. It never becomes a £0 job.
2. **Versions: `externalVersion` = your version of the order**, 1 then +1 on every change. That is the only number the
   till compares: a version not above the one it holds changes nothing. `rowVersion` can carry the same number, but the
   till never compares it (it stamps its own on every write, including when staff open the job). Please change your
   rule 1 to "the same `externalVersion` again is a no-op".
3. **Cancel: `op: U` with `status: cancelled`** (+ `cancelledAt`), as you have it. **`op: D` is refused.** It would remove
   the job without releasing its held parts.
4. **Release:** see the status line above. The till sends `X-SSPOS-App-Version` on every sync call, so you can check it
   against `WORKSHOP_CONNECTOR_MIN_TILL`.

## 2. Corrections for the portal side

a. **When the order locks.** Answer 409 `order_locked` as soon as a pushed `CustomerOrder` with the order's id arrives,
   **in any status**. Don't wait for a payment, collection, cancellation or sale. Opening the job on a till sale creates
   that customer order straight away, before any money is taken, and from then on the till refuses changes. With your
   current trigger, a mechanic's change made while the customer is at the counter would get 200 from you and then be
   refused by the till.

b. **The lock covers every change:** `PUT` of the order or a line, `DELETE` (cancel), and `PUT …/status/Complete`.
   Without that, a deposit taken at the till plus `paidElsewhere` = the whole total would count the money twice.

c. **What the till does with a cancel:**
   - Before the job is opened on a sale: the till marks it cancelled and releases the held parts. The order stays on
     the till as cancelled; it is not deleted.
   - After that (or once collected): the cancel is refused like any change, flagged once per version on the till's
     Sync status screen, and the shop refunds at the till.

d. **`ownership.json` is generated by the till**; please take ours from this folder rather than patching yours.
   `WorkshopOrder` is now `"hub"`, with its rules in a new section `hubSent`: a hub table the portal sends to one shop
   only, which that till applies by its own rules and never pushes. Your `branchAddressed` wording was right; it is in
   `hubSent` now.

e. **Till-owned members:** send them empty, as you do. The till ignores `customerOrderId`, `lockedByRegisterId`,
   `lockedAt`, `saleId`, `collectedAt`, `priceDifference`, `isReady`, `jobStatus`, `mechanicName`, `receivedAt`,
   `isOpen` and `isStarted`. A null `customerId` never clears a customer the till has matched itself.

f. **`workshop.connector_status`:** your values are right. The till never pushes it. It is in a new list
   `portalWrittenKeys` in `samples/settings-local-only.json`.

## 3. One new setting you need to know about: `workshop.job_source`

A shop only switches the workshop on at the till by entering its own BikeBook key, and the till then looks at BikeBook's
unpaid invoices every minute ("Direct mode"). So every shop that uses your connector also runs Direct mode, and both see
the same job. The new branch setting `workshop.job_source` decides which one makes **new** jobs. The shop sets it on the
till's Workshop integration card, and it comes up to you like the other `workshop.*` keys.

| `workshop.connector_status` | `workshop.job_source` | New jobs come from |
|---|---|---|
| `connected` or `failed` | `connector` (default) | **your rows only**; the till's own look makes no new jobs |
| `connected` or `failed` | `direct` | the till's own look; **a new row of yours is skipped, not flagged** |
| anything else | either | the till's own look |

In every case, a job already on the till keeps updating from the source that created it. A job is never on the till
twice:
- A row of yours for a job the till already holds from its own key (same `externalInvoiceId`, otherwise same
  `externalJobNumber`) is not added. With `connector` it is also flagged for the staff.
- The till's own look skips a job that came from you. **Please always fill
`externalInvoiceId` when BikeBook gives one.**

Because a shop on `direct` skips your new rows, please check this setting before you tell BikeBook a job reached the
shop's till.
