## Roles

For the personal data of your shoppers and staff that your tills send to this backoffice, **your business is the
controller** and **Switch & Save is the processor**.

## What the final agreement will cover

- Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing.
- Types of personal data (for example customer names, contact details, loyalty and account history, marketing
  consent, staff names and clock records) and categories of data subjects.
- Our duties as processor: act only on your documented instructions, confidentiality, security measures, help with
  data subject requests (the backoffice's customer data export and anonymise tools), breach notification,
  deletion or return of data at the end of the service, audits.
- Use of [sub-processors](/legal/subprocessors) and how we tell you about changes.
- International transfers.
